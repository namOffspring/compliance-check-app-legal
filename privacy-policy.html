This Privacy Policy explains how Compliance Check App ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our developer tools, including any integrations with third-party developer APIs.

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to withdraw previously granted API authorizations. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Security
